Privacy policy

Aurelens (formerly AutoGolden) is an XAU/USD market-data and research app operated by the individual developer Geruyang. For privacy requests, use our support page. This update changes the brand and presentation, not the app’s data handling.

Accounts, advertising and trading

The app does not connect bank, brokerage or payment accounts, execute trades or hold assets. It does not use advertising SDKs, track across apps or sell personal information. It does not request or store website login passwords for AI services.

Information on your device

Language, model selection, notification settings, manually entered costs and quantities, and recent conversations are stored in the app sandbox. Model API keys are stored in Apple Keychain. You can clear manual holdings in the app; clearing an API key and saving removes the corresponding Keychain item.

External services and transmission

The app makes functional network requests to public Tonghuashun market-data endpoints, the US Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) and GDELT. Requests to your selected OpenAI, DeepSeek or GLM service occur only after you initiate an Agent request or connection test and give explicit consent.

Agent requests may include your question, current market and indicator summaries, event or news summaries, and necessary manual-holding summaries. External services may receive standard network information, including IP address, request time and technical logs, and process it under their own policies. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information.

News requests

Since version 0.6.2 build 12, GDELT receives only preset gold-news keywords, not your questions, manual holdings or model API keys. It may still receive standard network information; we do not promise zero log retention. This describes build 12 and later and does not imply older builds received the change automatically.

AI requests still send your questions, analysis and necessary holding summaries after consent. API keys authenticate with the selected provider; requests may be associated with your provider account and are not anonymous. Retention and use depend on that provider’s applicable API terms and privacy policy.

Notifications and controls

Notifications are off by default and are used only after you enable watch-zone alerts and grant system permission. You can turn off live data, notifications and Agent data-sharing consent. iOS controls background refresh scheduling.

Requests and updates

For help accessing, deleting or correcting local information, contact us through the support page. Do not put API keys or financial-account details in public issues. Material changes to functionality or data handling will be reflected in this policy and its effective date.
